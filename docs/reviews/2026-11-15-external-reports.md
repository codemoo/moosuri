## Limits

This is one foreground document outcome with at most64 actual receipts and existing
chat size/budget limits. It does not add mission writes, external workflow learning,
browser/native-preview persistence or automatic refresh. External observations can
be stale; receipt identity establishes what was read, not the correctness of the
report. Local downloads cannot be remotely recalled. General live-account/device,
