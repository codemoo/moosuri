## Product changes and consolidation

- Compound routing ignores operation words inside well-formed quoted literals.
  A title containing `계산` no longer turns a simple save into a new research task.
  Korean particles after quotes and English contractions are covered.
- Artifact intent admission and target resolution share a dedicated module. Current
  explicit edit intent can use a bounded literal reference to the immediately
  preceding owned save receipt. Thread/session/epoch,30-minute age, invalidation,
  kind and unchanged revision remain required. New titles cannot become old IDs.
  Exact named matching reads at most two matches, and ambiguity never selects one.
  The verified target goes to the execution context so the model can read it
  directly. Existing literal-anchor and one-write/replay checks remain in force.
- Pending-question admission, local cancellation and reminder draft replies share
  their eligibility and finite cancellation grammar. A whole reply cancels only
  the immediate eligible question, with no inference or saved task/event deletion.
  Ambiguous corrections retain an already established still-missing field, its
  original request, useful prompt and expiry; they cannot invent authority.
- Monitor reuse and normal admission share expiry/withdrawal classification.
  Invalid old permission no longer swallows a new task's proposal. Publication,
  inbox and Push use one observation-validity gate, so withdrawn/deleted results
  cannot keep delivering stale notices. Valid partial reviews remain available.

## Bounds

This is not a broad unseen quality score. Public scripted workflows check specified
execution contracts; actual-model rechecks reuse known failed requests. Simple
recent-document references use only the immediately preceding receipt, not semantic
search through old conversations. Composite cancel-and-new-action interpretation
and arbitrary multi-field historical correction remain outside these bounded
routes. Existing explicit final-preview approval is still required for every mail
send. No new user approval step is introduced by these internal checks.
