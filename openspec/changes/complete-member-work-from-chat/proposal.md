# Complete member work from everyday conversation

## Why

On Linux, two differently worded requests for the same research produced two durable searches in one case and a text reply in the other. The member could see a source on a task card but could not refer to it in the next message. Direct observation of Muse use also showed short corrections, external research followed by preparation, human handoff and recurring checks. [Research and baseline](../../../docs/research/kiri-conversation-work-2026-11-25.md)

## What Changes

- Accept a member's request once, relate follow-ups to the correct work, and return results that open in the original thread.
- Carry current source evidence into replies and combined private reports. Keep independent requests and their worklogs addressable while the member continues chatting.
- Exercise connected research, applications, shopping, browser takeover, mail, calendar, recurring briefings and reusable preferences with synthetic counterparts of observed tasks. Record the member-facing completion boundary and provider receipts for each.

## Impact

Kiri member runtime and volume, shared model broker caller, account gateway, personal React client and the Linux member acceptance suite. Backend execution stays in Rust, the member store in SQLite, and model selection with the installation administrator. [Runtime design](../isolate-account-runtime/design.md)
