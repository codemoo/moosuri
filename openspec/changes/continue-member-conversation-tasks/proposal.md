# Continue member tasks in their originating conversation

## Why

Members can already revisit a local search. A request for several results needs a durable handoff, a way to direct a later message to one result, and a visible record of which steps produced it.

## What Changes

- Connect bounded memory and conversation searches to one durable originating chat turn and independent child tasks.
- Run accepted reads on the authorized member timer. Support targeted refinement, clarification, cancellation and verified procedure reuse.
- Publish current evidence and saved research reports through member-authorized reads. Stop browser work when repeated observations show no progress.
- Verify the six feature groups with synthetic member volumes and Linux browser scenarios. [Scenarios and worklog](../../../docs/reviews/2026-11-25-conversation-work.md)

## Impact

Kiri chat, personal tasks, member database migration 92 and the member browser. Relates to F09, F25, F26, F27 and F28.
