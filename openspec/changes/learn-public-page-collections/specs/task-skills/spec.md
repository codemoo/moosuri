## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Explicit bounded public-page collection
Newly admitted capable runs SHALL expose a two-to-four-page collection primitive
only with the exact bundled pin and configured public HTTP reads. Targets SHALL
be distinct supported HTTP(S) URL literals in the current user message or current
accepted owner mission task, in that literal order; missing, duplicate, extra,
historical or page-supplied URLs SHALL NOT authorize a read. Actual IO SHALL retain
existing DNS/address/redirect/size/timeout/no-credential boundaries.

#### Scenario: Collect and compare fresh pages
- **WHEN** an owner supplies two to four supported public URLs and asks for a comparison
- **THEN** each actual read has a separately charged dispatch and retained receipt
- **AND** the result preserves each requested and observed URL, title, time, excerpt, truncation or failure
- **AND** evidence collection never asserts semantic comparison success or whole-page coverage

### Requirement: Durable composite execution
Each collection SHALL share the original owner/run/step/time/tool budget and record
parent plus individual start/completion identities before dispatch. Exact slot and
completion replays SHALL not duplicate IO or charge. A previously started parent
with unknown completion SHALL return unknown without replay. Cancellation, pin
withdrawal, revised acceptance, owner/model/restore fences SHALL reject late reads
and output through existing contracts.

#### Scenario: Partial failure or lost dispatch acknowledgement
- **WHEN** one page fails or the worker loses a started collection acknowledgement
- **THEN** observed pages and individual failures remain distinct; failed/unknown work cannot qualify a learned recipe
- **AND** restart or replay never automatically refetches the original targets

### Requirement: Private structural qualification and fresh reuse
Qualification SHALL require two completed native collections with distinct canonical
targets and linked per-page receipts adopted in terminal answers. A route SHALL
retain only page count, hashes and opaque proof links. Fresh foreground or explicit
mission reuse SHALL read current-task targets; missions SHALL NOT train new proofs
or declare semantic completion.

#### Scenario: Learn A and B, then execute C
- **WHEN** two different finalized target sets provide actual adopted page evidence
- **THEN** the private route becomes ready and a new explicit third target set is read afresh
- **AND** prior target/body values do not enter discovery metadata or fresh tool arguments
- **AND** adoption requires each observed URL, title and exact short quote; reordered targets, replay or another owner's results cannot establish independent proof

#### Scenario: Withdraw a used recipe or training conversation
- **WHEN** a proof conversation is deleted or an actually used route is disabled/removed
- **THEN** existing owner-scoped qualification and transitive answer/mission withdrawal apply
- **AND** dependent recurring work requires its original exact usable pin and explicit recovery

### Requirement: Synthetic structural sharing
Sharing SHALL reconstruct collection count and original instructions with new
invented URL examples. Private URLs, page bodies, credentials, proof receipts and
training authority SHALL NOT cross into exported files or installed packages.
Installation SHALL NOT fetch example URLs or establish successful-use evidence.

#### Scenario: Export a privately trained collection
- **WHEN** an owner previews a shareable collection recipe
- **THEN** the existing four-file checked package contains only structure and repository-owned examples
- **AND** another installer must provide their own current targets and available read capability
