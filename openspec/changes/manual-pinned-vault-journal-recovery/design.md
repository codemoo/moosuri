## Source and trust

The operator supplies old live snapshot/head and full journal/head plus an explicit
independently retained SHA256 of the exact later receipt. Public recipient age
encryption supplies confidentiality, not authorship or latestness. Only selected
head coverage is claimed. Manual export requires existing healthy applied source
evidence and stopped writer admission; live captured-stage encryption is also
available. It never seals, initializes absent evidence or exports unlock secrets.

## Staged forward recovery

Authenticate both age EOFs, receipt pin and exact file/hash/metadata, then require
same source UUID, non-regressing sequence and exact old journal prefix bytes/head.
Later records reach only a private staged Database replay method. Actual storage
Prepare/Adopt/Cancel/Lifecycle rules enforce CAS/header/inventory/nonce semantics.
Close affects only a digest closure/head. Every generated sequence/head and final
journal bytes must match. New ciphertext namespace anchors grant no sessions.
Normal general crash-tail admission is unchanged; no schema change is needed.

## Publication and compatibility

Existing full semantic verification precedes latest pending cancellation/session
removal. A fresh UUID and distinct historical provenance bind snapshot and journal.
Exact rerun preserves new destination work. Different pins/occupied roots cannot
be adopted; plain restore cannot reinterpret a historical root. Previous formats
are unchanged. Automatic off-host receiver/current pin, hardware and zero-loss
acceptance remain separate.
