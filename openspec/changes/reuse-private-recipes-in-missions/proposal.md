# Reuse proven private local recipes in delegated work

## Why
Learned local procedures currently stop at foreground chat even though the owner can explicitly delegate the same read/calculation task. Connect that existing proven route to one-off and recurring missions.

## What Changes
Admit current owner-qualified local pins at creation; execute fresh inputs under original budgets; record actual recurring dependencies and visible exact receipts. Keep parcel and external writes excluded. Mission execution does not qualify new training proofs.

## Impact
P4/P5, existing Store/agent mission profiles and private recipe lifecycle. No schema, provider credentials, external effect authority or CI changes.
